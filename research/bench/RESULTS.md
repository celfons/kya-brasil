## Findings

- **Found by this benchmark, fixed in @kya-brasil/core 0.19.1.** Before: verifyCrl refused any CRL above about 1 240 entries ("Maximum ASN.1 node count exceeded": asn1js default maxNodes = 10 000 via @peculiar/x509 X509Crl), so the 77 000-entry SERPRO RFB v5 CRL could not be verified. Measured with core 0.19.0 on this machine: refused after 56 ms; largest accepted 1 244 entries (60 175 bytes), verifyCrl 109 ms median; the full 77k CRL parse+verify+index with asn1js maxNodes raised (reference outside the library): 3 658 ms median, 4 125 ms p95. After: core 0.19.1 parses the CRL with its own bounded DER reader (64 MiB, 2 000 000 entries), indexes serials by offset and decodes an entry only on lookup; the signature is verified over the TBSCertList bytes with Web Crypto.
